Explore Kolhapur

A quick survival guide for our guests spending time in Kolhapur!

🏛️ Culture & Sights

Mahalaxmi Temple: 7th-century Shaktipeeth of the powerful goddess.

New Palace Museum: Historic Maratha architecture and royal heritage.

👡 Shopping Spot

Chappal Galli: Right behind Mahalaxmi Temple for authentic handcrafted Kolhapuri leather & velvet footwear.

🍛 Food Spots

Akka Masoor Thali: Slow-cooked spicy red lentil delicacy at Hotel Royal.

Misal Pav: Authentic spicy Kolhapuri Misal at Phadtare Misal Kendra.
